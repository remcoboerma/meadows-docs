# By Interest

The pages above are organised by *where* you are (classroom, workshop, hackathon, home). This one is organised by *what you care about* — because people lean in when they see their own interests laid out.

| Your interest | What MEADOWS offers you | Start here |
|---|---|---|
| **Aspiring programmer / vibe coder** | Write your first talking bot; grow it into something that does a job | [First bot tutorial](../tutorials/first-bot.md) |

| **Social sciences & civics** | Simulate belief systems meeting world events; observe opinion dynamics emerge | [Classroom](classroom.md) |
| **Ethics, privacy & digital citizenship** | Build the profiling machine in a sandbox to truly understand it | [Concepts](../concepts/index.md) |
