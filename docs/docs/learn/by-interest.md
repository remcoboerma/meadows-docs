# By Interest

The pages above are organised by *where* you are (classroom, workshop, hackathon, home). This one is organised by *what you care about* — because people lean in when they see their own interests laid out.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-console:{ .lg .middle } **Aspiring programmer / vibe coder**

    ---

    Write your first talking bot; grow it into something that actually does a job. Learn by making a thing that lives among other things.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: First bot tutorial](../tutorials/first-bot.md)

-   :material-server-network:{ .lg .middle } **Backend & distributed systems**

    ---

    Message bus, routing, RPC, stateful reconnection, JWT auth, capability scoping — the real concepts of networked software, with the deployment cliff removed.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Architecture overview](../architecture/overview.md)

-   :material-brain:{ .lg .middle } **Machine learning & data science**

    ---

    [Labeling](../architecture/labeling.md) as a live classification playground; embeddings and novelty scoring; A/B-test whether applied labels are actually any good; train on patterns that emerge from real conversation.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Labeling walkthrough](../tutorials/labeling-walkthrough.md)

-   :material-chart-timeline:{ .lg .middle } **Systems thinkers**

    ---

    The [dual-context bot](../concepts/index.md#the-dual-context-bot), delays, differing rates of change, feedback loops, and Meadows' leverage points — made pokeable. Decentralization as a [tunable set of axes](../architecture/design-principles.md#decentralization-is-a-spectrum-not-a-switch), not a stance.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Design principles](../architecture/design-principles.md)

-   :material-sitemap:{ .lg .middle } **Software, systems & information architects**

    ---

    MEADOWS is a case study in [architecture as deliberate tradeoff](../architecture/design-principles.md#architecture-as-deliberate-tradeoff). Decentralize meaning and governance; centralize coordination and trust; cap federation on purpose. Patterns that recur in organisations, institutions, cities, and ecosystems.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Architecture overview](../architecture/overview.md)

-   :material-account-group:{ .lg .middle } **Social sciences & civics**

    ---

    Simulate belief systems meeting world events; observe opinion dynamics, consensus, and polarisation emerge; [governance without a central authority](../architecture/design-principles.md#2-trusted-operator-assumption) as a live model.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Classroom](classroom.md)

-   :material-shield-check:{ .lg .middle } **Ethics, privacy & digital citizenship**

    ---

    Build the profiling machine in a sandbox to truly understand it. Sit with the "means versus ticks" line. Debate self-hosting, data ownership, and who gets to see what — from the inside.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Concepts](../concepts/index.md)

-   :material-newspaper:{ .lg .middle } **Media & information literacy**

    ---

    RAG, summarisation, source evaluation, and the discipline of catching an AI being confidently wrong.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Classroom](classroom.md)

-   :material-school:{ .lg .middle } **Teachers & educators**

    ---

    Author your own subject-specific bots without being an engineer; learn AI facilitation from peers by watching them work; reuse the archive of past sessions.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Workshop](workshop.md)

-   :material-book-open-variant:{ .lg .middle } **Language & humanities**

    ---

    Bots that discuss texts, label rhetorical and argumentative moves, and track how the meaning of a conversation shifts over time.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Labeling walkthrough](../tutorials/labeling-walkthrough.md)

-   :material-earth:{ .lg .middle } **Global citizenship / SDG-minded learners**

    ---

    The whole platform *is* SDG 4 (Quality Education) in action. Model SDG tensions as systems — climate action (SDG 13) as a belief-versus-event dynamic, or peace and institutions (SDG 16) through governance that has no central enforcer.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Learn](index.md)
